---
title: Built-in module
description: A module included in Deckhouse Platform. Its release cycle is tied to the release cycle of the platform.
lang: en
---

Built-in modules are delivered together with a [Deckhouse Platform release](glossary.html#dp-release) and are updated with it. Which of them are enabled by default depends on the [module bundle](glossary.html#module-bundle) and the [edition](glossary.html#edition). The other kind of modules are [modules from a source](glossary.html#module-from-source), whose releases do not depend on the platform releases.

For details, see [Deckhouse Platform module development](architecture/module-development/).
