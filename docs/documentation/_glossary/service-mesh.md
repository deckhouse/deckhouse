---
title: Service mesh
description: A means of managing and configuring network interactions between applications within a cluster.
lang: en
---

The service mesh is provided by the [`istio`](/modules/istio/) module. Within one cluster it is used, for example, for the Circuit Breaker and Canary Deployment patterns. [Federation](glossary.html#federation) and [multicluster](glossary.html#multicluster) are built on top of it.

For details, see [Inter-cluster cooperation](admin/configuration/network/alliance/).
