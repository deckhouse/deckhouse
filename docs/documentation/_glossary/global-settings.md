---
title: Global settings
description: Deckhouse Platform settings that apply to the whole cluster. They are stored in the [ModuleConfig](reference/api/cr.html#moduleconfig) named global.
lang: en
---

Global settings can be thought of as a special `global` [module](glossary.html#module) that cannot be disabled. The settings of other modules are stored in separate ModuleConfig objects named after the module.

For details, see [Global settings](reference/api/global.html).
