---
title: Stronghold lease
description: Temporary access to secrets and resources in the Deckhouse Stronghold secrets store, identified by a lease ID.
lang: en
---

Leases are managed in the "Leases" section of the Stronghold web interface, where a lease is found by its ID. Together with access control policies, authentication methods and entities, leases define who may use the [secrets store](glossary.html#secrets-store) and for how long.

For details, see [Secret management web interface](user/web/stronghold.html).
