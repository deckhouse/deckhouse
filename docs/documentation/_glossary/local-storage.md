---
title: Local storage
description: Storage on the local disks of a node. It reduces network latency and gives higher performance than storage accessed over the network.
lang: en
---

Local storage is one of the [software-defined storage](glossary.html#sds) types and is provided by the [`sds-local-volume`](/modules/sds-local-volume/) module, which supports the LVM and LVMThin modes. It is effective in test environments and EDGE clusters.

For details, see [Setting up local storage based on LVM](admin/configuration/storage/sds/lvm-local.html).
